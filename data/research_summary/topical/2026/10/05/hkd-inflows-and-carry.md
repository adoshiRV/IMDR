# HKD — equity inflows, the missing demand, and the forward carry book

**Two questions, one setup.**

1. Given equity performance, what are HK inflows doing, and why are they not producing the HKD demand they did in 2025? Is there a risk spot is pulled lower without the band being tested?
2. What are T/N dynamics telling us, and is the 3m/9m forward the right expression of the carry trade everyone is in?

**Decision lens.** Does the 2026 inflow picture support holding long USD/HKD into the 7.85 weak-side CU, and is the forward curve the right vehicle for it — or does the flow path produce a loss with no liquidity event to reset the trade?

**Answer in one line.** The inflows are arriving, and they are exactly large enough to stop the carry trade reaching 7.85 and exactly too small to pull it away. The 3m/9m book is hedged against the wrong downside.

## Part 1 — The missing HKD demand

### 1.1 The premise, corrected

HK equity performance is not strong. That is the answer, not the puzzle.

HSI trails SPX by **19.3pp** YTD; −2.6% on 2 Oct alone.

### 1.2 Gross flow is a record. Net flow is roughly nil.

| Channel | 2025 | 2026 YTD | For HKD |
|---|---|---|---|
| Southbound Connect net buying | **US$180bn** (FY) | **US$56bn** (to 2 Oct) | **buys HKD** |
| HK IPO proceeds | < HK$388bn (FY) | **HK$388bn / US$49.4bn** (9M, record since 1980) | **sells HKD** |
| Secondary placements | — | Alibaba HK$80bn/US$10.2bn (26 Aug), Innolight US$7bn, Zhongji US$7bn, CoreX US$4bn, SHEIN US$2.8bn | **sells HKD** |

Southbound is running **US$6.2bn/month against US$15.0bn/month in 2025 — 41% of the pace** — while issuance on the other side is the largest in 46 years.

**Mechanism** (BofA desk, 25–26 Aug, on Alibaba): a placement is funded by (A) selling HKD assets, (B) borrowing HKD, or (C) selling USD/HKD spot. Only (C) is HKD demand and it is a fraction of the total. The issuer then converts out — *"Alibaba's got to convert the HKD proceeds back to CNY or USD… they don't have big AI projects to invest in Hong Kong."* **Net, a HK equity raise is a USD/HKD buy.** HKMA named conversion of equity and bond issuance proceeds as a driver of the June-2025 weak-side move.

### 1.3 Four reasons 2025 is not repeating

| # | Channel | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Southbound pace collapsed** | US$56bn 9M26 vs US$180bn FY25. HK daily turnover HK$202bn Sep (HK$253bn Aug, HK$350bn early Jul). Short-sale ratio **19.1% Sep** vs 15.3% Aug, +1.8SD vs the 14.6% mean since 2014 (JPM, 1 Oct) |
| 2 | **Secondary → primary** | 2025 was buying existing shares (pure HKD demand); 2026 is record primary issuance that converts out |
| 3 | **Beijing throttled the channel** | CSRC penalties to three offshore brokerages (22 May); HKMA/SFC account-opening guidelines; **State Council Order No. 837 on Outbound Investment, effective 1 Jul 2026**; 20% tax enforcement on offshore trusts/insurance (HSBC, 13 Jul) |
| 4 | **The system absorbs it without tightening** | HKD LDR **69.3% Aug** (70.7 Jul, 71.5 Jun) vs 90.3% end-2019 — five straight monthly falls. HKD deposits +9.8% y/y vs HKD loans +2.0%. CNH deposits +21.3%, CNH LDR 96% — HKD loans migrating to CNH (SCB, 30 Sep) |

Plus **FINI** (HKEX, 2023), which closed the IPO-subscription lockup channel that used to spike HIBOR. Tested live: HK$46bn of IPOs in one week moved T/N 0.5 pip and faded in days.

HSBC desk, 3 Oct: *"Equity turnover continues to trend lower. **The recent HIBOR firmness has been more Fed-driven than locally driven.**"*

### 1.4 The band is doing nothing, and that is the finding

| | May 2025 | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Aggregate Balance | HK$44.6bn → **HK$174.1bn** (7–9 May) | **HK$53.7–54.0bn, unchanged 14 months** |
| Strong-side injection | **+HK$129.5bn** | none |
| Weak-side drains | −HK$119.8bn, 27 Jun–15 Aug | **none in 2026** |
| T/N carry | −11.4 (May), **−14.0 pips/day** (Jun–Jul) | **−3.2 to −4.3** (Aug–Sep) |
| Band crossing | 7.75 → 7.85 in **under 7 weeks** | 7.7811 → 7.8472 over **9 months, never arrived** |

**The grind is decelerating.** Monthly average USD/HKD: Jan 7.7960 → Feb 7.8165 (+205 pips) → Mar (+105) → Apr (+69) → … → Aug 7.8429 (+18) → **Sep 7.8433 (+4)** → Oct 7.8472. September's southbound was **6× August's and the monthly average moved +0.4 pips.** The whole of September traded a **70-pip range**. Spot is 2.8 pips from the weak CU and has been "nearly there" since July.

### 1.5 The risk is the modal path, not the tail

**Path 1 — drain-driven.** Weak CU → AB drained → HIBOR spikes → points collapse → crowded long unwinds. Spot fell **−0.86%** from the 7.85 pin (2 Jul 2025) to the Sep trough and **never touched 7.75** (low 7.7686).

**Path 2 — flow-driven, no CU.** Inflow absorbs the carry bid → spot drifts → AB unchanged → HIBOR unchanged → **points widen rather than narrow**. Observed 1–10 Jul 2026: US$5.7bn southbound + ~US$7bn placement + US$6bn IPO took spot 7.8440 → 7.8340, **3x12s −425 → −470, 1y outright 7.7770 → 7.7640 in two days** (BofA desk).

**The crowd is in 1Y outrights and the 6x12, not 3m/9m per se.** 3M is the hedge leg — Nomura's 126d z-score for 3M carry is **+0.84**, the only tenor materially above its own average, i.e. where carry has compressed most. That compression is the insurance premium: 3M spans the Dec IMM and the drain window. **On every risk-adjusted measure 1M–2M dominates and 9M/12M is worst.**

### 2.3 What the two paths cost — the core finding

**Path 1 — drain-driven. 2 Jul → 29 Sep 2025:**

| | spot | 1M | 3M | 6M | 9M | 1Y |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MTM | **−0.859%** | −0.612% | −0.313% | −0.146% | **−0.031%** | **+0.019%** |

Spot fell 86bp and **the 1Y outright made money**. The HIBOR spike collapses the points, which offsets the spot fall almost exactly. **Longer = more immune.**

**Path 2 — flow-driven, no CU. 22 → 28 Sep 2026:**

| | spot | 1M | 3M | 6M | 9M | 1Y |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MTM | **+0.018%** | +0.019% | −0.016% | −0.037% | **−0.047%** | **−0.048%** |

**Spot went up and the 9M/1Y lost 4.7–4.8bp in six sessions**, because the points widened by more than spot rose. Same signature 1–10 Jul 2026: spot −4.7bp, 9M −7.0bp, 1Y −6.3bp.

**Across 2026 (2 Jan → 2 Oct):** spot +0.703%, 1M +0.687%, 3M +0.693%, **9M +0.586%, 1Y +0.551%.** The 9M/1Y **underperformed spot by 12–15bp and a rolled 1M by ~10–14bp** through nine months of the trade *working*.

**So, precisely:** the 3m/9m book is well hedged against the drain path and badly exposed to the flow path. The hedge learned in 2025 is **conditional on the CU being triggered** — and the risk case is defined by it *not* being triggered.

Held to maturity the 9M at 7.78804 pays +0.80% at 7.85 and **−0.49% at 7.75**. Marked to market, a May-2025 repeat (spot 7.7550, 9M points −632) marks it at ~7.692, **−1.24%**. Against +0.80% upside, MTM risk/reward is **~0.6:1 against** — and VaR stops bind on the MTM.

### 2.6 What is priced

|---|---|---|---|
| Spot 3M (Oct–Jan) | 3.265% | 4.017% | −75bp |

**UBS says HK$20bn.** A baseline difference, not an error: UBS measures 1m HKD (3.00–3.25% in Q1) against their *seasonal* profile, which has HIBOR falling 50–100bp in Q1. **Both are true — the market prices only ~HK$2–5bn of excess tightening relative to the Fed, and prices out most of the usual Q1 seasonal easing.** The seasonal component is the larger part, which is why UBS, BofA's desk and SCB all want to receive the forward-forwards and why Citi warns the front end is too high if the band is never tested.

## Binding deciders

1. **The first weak-side touch** — not the level, the *size and repetition* of the drain. SCB <HK$10bn, UBS HK$15–20bn, BofA Research HIBOR to 6% below HK$50bn AB. One touch confirms consensus; a second within a fortnight breaks the receive-the-forwards trade at every house.
2. **October southbound.** September's HK$61bn did not move spot. HK$80bn+ with spot still pinned kills the flow path and the band test happens. Spot backing up on HK$60bn months puts us in the risk case, with the 9M book bleeding and no liquidity event to reset it.
